Note: Mu'akkadah (emphasized Sunnah) should not be skipped. Ghair Mu'akkadah (non-emphasized Sunnah) is highly rewardable but optional (e.g. 4 Rakats before Asr and 4 Rakats before Isha).

📋 Conditions (Shuroot) of Salah

  • Islam & Sanity: Must be a Muslim and of sound mind.
  • Cleanliness (Taharah): Body, clothing, and place of prayer must be clean. Perform Wudu or Ghusl.
  • Covering Awrah: Men must cover navel to knee; women must cover everything except face and hands.
  • Facing Qibla: Must face the direction of Kaabah in Makkah.
  • Time: The specific prayer time must have entered.
  • Intention (Niyyah): Sincere intention in the heart to pray.
